# Privacy

ModernRegedit collects no data. The app runs locally: it reads and writes the Windows Registry on your machine and sends nothing to any server. No telemetry, no ads, no tracking.

The Microsoft Store handles its own data per [Microsoft's privacy policy](https://privacy.microsoft.com/).
